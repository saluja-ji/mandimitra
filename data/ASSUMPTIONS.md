# Data and assumptions

## Bundled sample
`sample/demo_prices.csv` is generated by a deterministic script embedded in the build notes and has `data_status=SYNTHETIC_DEMO`. It is NOT official market data, not live, and not evidence about actual prices. The sample is provided only to make the software runnable and testable offline.

## Recommendation assumptions
- Modal price is expressed in Indian rupees per quintal (100 kg).
- Gross revenue estimate = forecast price × quantity in quintals.
- Transport cost is a user-entered total-trip cost per kilometre multiplied by the market distance. It is a simplification; users must define it consistently.
- Value loss per 100 km is a user-entered scenario assumption, not a sourced biological spoilage model. The app applies it linearly with distance and caps it at 100%.
- Forecast = arithmetic mean of the most recent N observations per market, not a trained or validated advanced AI model.
- The evaluation compares the moving-average forecast with a last-observation baseline using chronological one-step-ahead predictions. The synthetic sample cannot establish real-world forecast performance.

## What real data should replace
Use an official download from the Government of India's Open Government Data platform / AGMARKNET, record retrieval date and licence/terms, retain original source metadata, and document cleaning. Do not scrape a site if its terms prohibit it. Data portal: https://www.data.gov.in/catalog/current-daily-price-various-commodities-various-markets-mandi
